## Breath of the Wild: An "Escape Plan" from the City

> "If coding is for survival, then camping is for finding your soul."

Between the "Concrete Jungle" of Hong Kong and the "Code Factory" of Shenzhen, I choose to "Format" myself during holidays and dive into the embrace of nature.

---

### 🏕️ CNY 2024: An "Expedition" to the Wumeng Grassland

While the whole world was watching the Spring Festival Gala and snatching Red Packets, my friends and I hit the road from Shenzhen, driving thousands of kilometers straight to the **Wumeng Grassland** in Panzhou, Guizhou.

*   **The "Cultivation" Experience**:
    At an altitude of 2800m, surrounded by nothing but thick white mist. It was freezing, but there were no Slack notifications, no production incidents to worry about. At that moment, I was just a "digital nomad" cultivating inner peace in the wild.
*   **Wild Chef Live**:
    Frying eggs in sub-zero temperatures is truly an art. Despite the shivering hands, that sizzling hot breakfast tasted better than anything you could find in a high-end restaurant.
    ![cooking](/img/camping/cooking.jpg)

---

### 🐄 Run! The "Original Residents" of the Grassland

In the Wumeng Grassland, the cattle are the true masters.

We pitched our tents next to the herds, "talked" to the cows in the mist, and even played hide-and-seek with them. In the face of nature, social status is meaningless. The only difference between you and a cow is: they are eating grass, and you are eating cup noodles.

---

### 🏮 Mental Exhaustion? Non-existent

The best part of camping is "Simplifying" a complex life:
1.  **Finding Water**: Survival instinct.
2.  **Making Fire**: Primitive joy.
3.  **Stargazing**: Spiritual massage.

When you sit by the campfire watching the flames dance, you realize that the most important things in life have always been right beside you.

---

### 🔙 Reset & Return

Every time after camping, the first thing I want to do is sleep for a whole day before going back to face the endless bugs. But it's exactly because of these "Breaths of the Wild" that I have the energy to keep tapping out my world on the keyboard.
